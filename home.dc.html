The Studio

I build data driven solutions for the people products usually overlook, with a background in supply chain BI and operations where I translate raw data into business and operations insights.

My biggest strength is awareness, grounded in respect for human dignity — I invest in understanding every stakeholder of a project, and I care just as much about the people I work alongside, committing to be aware and considerate of everyone I build with.

Every voice deserves to be heard, so I keep asking who benefits and who bears the burden at every step. The rest is taste: knowing what to add, what to leave out, and where the balance lives.

Rent Splitter

Splitting a household bill fairly is rarely one calculation. Rent scales with room size, utilities split evenly, parking belongs to whoever owns the car, and someone always owes a one-off. Most tools force all of it into a single method, so the arithmetic ends up in a group chat.

Outcome

4

Split methods per line item — equal, square footage, percentage, or assigned to one payer — plus manual adjustments.

Every line

Traceable back to the expense it came from, so a disputed total can be settled by looking rather than arguing.

Shareable

Saved splits open in a read-only view for roommates without an account. Groups and auth run on Firebase.

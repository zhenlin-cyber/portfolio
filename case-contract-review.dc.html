Contract Review

Schools sign data processing agreements they do not have the legal capacity to read. The clauses that matter most — breach notification windows, sub-processor rights, governing law — sit twenty pages deep in language written by the vendor's counsel. PolicyLens reads any Canvas or EdTech DPA against fifteen California laws and ethics frameworks, surfaces every gap, and returns remediation language a legal team can use.

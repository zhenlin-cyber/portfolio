Raw Material Batchboard & Inventory Dashboard

The Oracle-to-SAP cutover cost the Novato site its view of raw material readiness. Tracking scattered across SAP, LIMS, spreadsheets, and email, and Supply Chain, QC, and QA were each working from a different partial picture.

I unified SAP purchase order, goods receipt, batch, and usage decision data with LIMS sampling, testing, and closure records into one Power BI lifecycle row per batch. QC testing was split from QA release so each function could see its own step and its hand-offs, and stage-level cycle timing was added across goods receipt, sampling, closure, and usage decision.

Outcome

446

Batches visible in one lifecycle view — 389 awaiting receipt, 25 over process.

7

Lifecycle stages tracked, across three cycle-time segments.

1,798

Locators mapped at Plant 1000 against 262 specced materials.

2

Automated placement-discrepancy checks, ready for FM Global review.

Both dashboards were people problems as much as data problems. The information existed, but it lived in different systems and different teams, and much of the process knowledge lived only in people's heads. I reviewed the logic with Supply Chain, QC, QA, and Operations throughout, keeping the rules transparent so the prototypes can scale into governed Power BI reports.
